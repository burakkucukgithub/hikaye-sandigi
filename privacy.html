Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Hikaye Sandigi ("the App") is a personal tool that schedules and publishes the owner's own videos to the owner's own TikTok account.

Information we access

When the owner logs in with TikTok, the App receives an access token and basic profile information (such as display name). It uses these only to publish the owner's videos.

How information is used and stored

The access token is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party, and the App has no other users.

Data retention and deletion

The owner can delete the stored token at any time and can revoke the App's access in TikTok settings (Security and permissions).

Children

The App is not directed to children and does not collect data from them.
